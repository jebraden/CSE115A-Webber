A walk through the garden

An ordinary morning, a quiet path, and a few things worth keeping.

The path begins beneath an old oak tree. Along its edges, small plants find room between the stones. Fallen leaves settle in the cracks, and the light moves slowly across the path.

What the page remembers

The garden changes with each visit. What seemed bare last week is now covered with small leaves; a familiar corner catches the morning sun.

Some things are easier to notice when you slow down.

The air warms as the morning goes on. At the far end of the garden, the path turns back toward the gate. There is no grand conclusion, just a record of a place and a moment.

Notes for later

Near the gate, a bench sits in the shade. It is a good place to pause before heading home.
